# Fidelity Live-Shape Mismatch Ledger

All live receipts are redacted or structural. Raw Fidelity exports remain
gitignored and were not copied into tracked files.

| ID | Severity | Synthetic claim | Live reality | Downstream impact | Recommended fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FID-LS-001 | `block` | The app has investment tables for holdings/activity, and synthetic Fidelity writes `positions_ledger`, `investment_holdings`, and `portfolio_snapshots` (`scripts/dummy_data/generator.py:1204`, `:1275`, `:1284`). | Current live ingest parses/reconstructs data but `persist_to_db` records only the SPAXX cash balance via `record_balance` (`scripts/ingest_fidelity_history.py:598-636`); connector `_run_ingest` calls that same path (`extractors/fidelity_connector.py:499-517`). | Live Fidelity holdings, activity, performance, cost basis, lots, dividends, and transfer flows do not reach the tables read by Investments. | Add a Fidelity live writer that persists `positions_ledger`, `investment_holdings`, and `portfolio_snapshots` from the CSV parse before using the app for live trust. |
| FID-LS-002 | `gap` | Synthetic Fidelity emits a fixed allocation and only `INITIAL_BASELINE`/`BUY` rows at a fixed price (`scripts/dummy_data/generator.py:1078-1085`, `:1131-1198`). | Redacted history fixtures cover `YOU BOUGHT`, `REINVESTMENT`, `DIVIDEND RECEIVED`, `CAP GAIN`, EFT in/out, and `EXPIRED`. | FID-LS-003 | `gap` | Synthetic Fidelity snapshots use no brokerage cash balance in the canonical allocation (`scripts/dummy_data/generator.py:1275-1282`). | Live positions include SPAXX as a money-market sweep with blank cost-basis fields; history includes SPAXX dividend and reinvestment pairs. | UI/account totals can misrepresent the equity-vs-cash split if SPAXX is treated as a normal equity holding or omitted. | Keep SPAXX/FDRXX as cash equivalents in holdings, allocation, performance, and tax-lot views. |
| FID-LS-004 | `block` | Synthetic Fidelity cash contributions are checking debits described as `FIDELITY EFT TRANSFER` and linked to one primary ledger row (`scripts/dummy_data/generator.py:1083`, `:1241-1247`; `tests/test_trusted_seed.py:106-120`). | Live history action is `Electronic Funds Transfer Received (Cash)` or `Electronic Funds Transfer Paid (Cash)`. | FID-LS-005 | `block` | Synthetic dividend flows depend on cash transactions categorized exactly as `Investment Income` (AI-016: `docs/data-lineage/ACTION_ITEMS.md:192-204`; flow matcher: `dal/reports/flow.py:557-620`). | Live parser classifies `DIVIDEND RECEIVED` and `CAP GAIN` as `DIVIDEND`, but it does not emit a transaction row or category. | FID-LS-006 | `block` | Cost basis is available to holdings/lots through `investment_holdings.cost_basis` and `positions_ledger.cost_basis_dec` (`dal/investments.py:56-94`, `:193-272`). | Positions CSV has `Cost Basis Total` and `Average Cost Basis`; connector currently sums `Cost Basis Total` into `loan_details` (`extractors/fidelity_connector.py:459-472`) rather than per-holding investment rows. | Investments UI can show missing/wrong cost basis and gain/loss for live Fidelity positions. | Persist per-position basis to `investment_holdings.cost_basis`; persist lot-forming trade/reinvestment basis to `positions_ledger.cost_basis_dec` when evidence exists. |
